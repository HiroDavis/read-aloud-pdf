# Privacy Policy — Read Aloud PDF (Kokoro)

**Effective date:** August 19, 2026

Read Aloud PDF reads PDF documents aloud using a text-to-speech voice that
runs entirely on your own computer.

## Data collection

This extension collects **no data whatsoever**. It has no server, no
accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, and no error reporting.

- **Your documents never leave your device.** PDFs are parsed and voiced
  locally in your browser. No document content, filename, or metadata is
  transmitted anywhere.
- **No personal information** is collected, stored, or shared.
- **No browsing activity** is observed or recorded.

## Network access

The extension makes exactly one kind of network request: on first use it
downloads the open-source Kokoro voice model (weight data files, ~310 MB)
from huggingface.co, and caches it locally so subsequent use works offline.
This request contains no personal data or document content. If you ask the
reader to open a PDF by URL, your browser fetches that PDF directly; no
third party is involved.

## Local storage

Reading preferences (voice, speed) and your last reading position per
document are stored locally in your browser and never transmitted.

## Changes

Any future change to this policy will be published at this URL and in the
extension's listing before taking effect.
